1. Origins

AE Industrial Partners (aerospace/defense private equity firm) roll-up:

  • 2020: acquires NuWave, PCI Strategic Management, ProModel Government Services
  • Feb 2021: NuWave + PCI merge → BigBear.ai ("decision dominance")
  • Apr 2022: rest of ProModel (~$16.1M)
  • Feb 29, 2024: Pangiam (vision AI / digital identity; McAleenan's company), all-stock
  • Dec 31, 2025: Ask Sage (~$250M headline / ~$272M consideration)
  • Jan 2026: CargoSeer (~$5M-scale customs/border AI)

SPAC: GigCapital4 merger closed Dec 7, 2021; NYSE BBAI from Dec 8, 2021; pro forma EV ~$1.378B.

Mission then: DoD/IC decision dominance (AI/ML, cyber, data science, systems integration). Now: "edge AI-powered decision intelligence" for national security, supply chain, digital identity + GenAI via Ask Sage. HQ McLean, VA.

Risks: ATM relapse; concentration/federal funding; GFIM damage; GenAI commoditization; litigation/ICFR; valuation ~9–10x TTM sales; politics/procurement reform.
