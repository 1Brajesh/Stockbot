1. Origins

  • Founded Nov 2017 at SF venture studio Atomic by Andrew Dudum, Hilary Coles, Jack Abraham, Joe Spector; seed from Forerunner, Thrive. Wedge: ED + hair loss for millennial men; Hers followed.
  • SPAC with Oaktree Acquisition Corp announced Sep/Oct 2020; closed Jan 20, 2021; ~$1.6B equity value, ~$280M proceeds incl. PIPE. Dudum Class V high-vote shares (~90% vote at close; 175 votes/share).
  • Then: narrow US men's Rx, generics. Now: multi-specialty + GLP-1 + hormones + labs; US + UK/EU + Canada + AU/JP; "everyday health companion" + AI clinical engine (company claim).
  • Skeptical read: compounding GLP-1 boom looked opportunistic; regulators saw marketing outrunning the law.

2. Products / model

Categories: sexual health (anchor); hair/derm (Hers nearing $1B claimed); mental health; weight loss oral + GLP-1 (compounded → branded pivot Mar 2026); testosterone/menopause (2025); labs (Quest partnership + YourBio); international (ZAVA, Livewell, Eucalyptus). Model: subscription; async intake → affiliated providers → own/partner pharmacy; 503A pharmacies (OH, AZ) + MedisourceRx 503B (~Sep 2024); personalized dosing. Peers: Ro (more clinical), LifeMD (primary care), Noom/WW (coaching), Novo/Lilly direct. HIMS edge = brand, breadth, fulfillment, not unique drug access. Contested: FDA warning letters Sep 9, 2025 (misbranded compounded semaglutide); FTC + CA/UT suit Jul 29, 2026 (health data to Meta/Snap; billing/cancellation ROSCA). Outcomes (~22 lb oral / ~29 lb GLP-1 year 1; >95% testosterone responders) are company check-in data, not RCTs.
