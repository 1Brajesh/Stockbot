Executive summary

Verdict: Speculative Hold / size small. Real wide-bandgap (WBG) power-semiconductor company with credible GaN IC IP and a SiC franchise (GeneSiC), pivoting from mobile chargers to AI data-center / grid / industrial high-power ("Navitas 2.0"). Technology is not fake; sequential revenue is rebuilding from a brutal 2025 collapse. But the public narrative (NVIDIA 800 VDC, explosive AI content) runs ahead of booked revenue, founders are gone, dilution has been heavy, and the stock prices a future Street only sees as mid-single-digit FY2026 growth off a crushed base. At ~$3.1B market cap on ~$37M TTM sales, this is a story stock with a cash war chest, not a proven scale business. High-beta option on WBG adoption in AI power, not a quality compounder.

One-liner: Real chips, weak P&L, rich multiple, new operators, litigated neighborhood — sized as a speculative sleeve, not a core holding.

1. Origins

  • Founded ~2013–2014 by Gene Sheridan + Dan Kinzer (ex–International Rectifier / HRL); started in a trailer at HRL Malibu; HQ Torrance, CA.
  • Mission: replace silicon power with GaN (later + SiC) for denser, more efficient conversion ("Electrify Our World").
  • Originally GaN power IC pure-play. Aug 15, 2022: acquired GeneSiC (~$100M cash + ~24.9M shares + earnout) for high-voltage SiC.
  • SPAC: Live Oak Acquisition Corp. II; closed Oct 19, 2021; Nasdaq NVTS Oct 20, 2021; ~$1.04B EV at deal.
  • Then: mobile/consumer chargers, GaNFast. Now ("Navitas 2.0"): de-emphasize mobile; push AI data centers, grid, industrial; dual GaN + HV/UHV SiC.

Skeptical read: Classic SPAC-era WBG narrative. Early revenue real but low-ASP charger volume. Founder-led growth → operator-led turnaround + AI story.

3. Leadership

  • Chris Allexandre — CEO since Sep 1, 2025: ex-Renesas SVP/GM Power (~$2.5B), CSMO; prior IDT, NXP, Fairchild/onsemi, TI. Operator DNA; Renesas sued him/Navitas for trade secrets (Jul 2026).
  • Tonya Stevens — CFO since Mar 30, 2026: Lattice CAO / interim CFO; Intel; CPA.
  • Gene Sheridan — founder CEO exited Aug 31, 2025 ($2.4M transition payments); no longer director.
  • Dan Kinzer — co-founder CTO/COO resigned May 2025; left board; advisory on GaN.
  • Ranbir Singh — GeneSiC founder; resigned board Jun 9, 2026; was ~6–8% holder, stake reduced.
  • Todd Glickman — longtime CFO mutual exit Feb 2026.

Board: Chair Richard Hendrix (Live Oak SPAC lineage). Mix of VC, SPAC sponsors, activist-ish capital, newer semi ops. Founder representation: none.

Legal: Wolfspeed v. Navitas (patent, Jul 2026); Renesas v. Navitas + CEO (trade secrets, Jul 2026); Navitas v. Renesas (patent countersuit); governance turbulence 2025.

Leadership read: New CEO is power-semi commercial operator, not founder-scientist. May help GTM — or become litigation/distraction tax.

4. Passion vs song-and-dance

Operator signals: R&D still >$13M/quarter on ~$10M revenue; GaNSafe + SiC Gen-3; reference PSUs; explicit pipeline disclaimer (good); Glassdoor ~4.0/5 but tiny sample.

Hype signals: Reference designs ≠ design wins ≠ revenue; two ~19% layoffs; 2024 "$450M design wins / $2.4B pipeline" color aged poorly into 2025 revenue crash; AI/NVIDIA multi-vendor and 2027-weighted.

Call: More than pure hype — silicon and customers exist — but marketing has often outrun the P&L. Passion shifted from founder mission to turnaround + AI TAM storytelling.

6. Future

Catalysts: Hyperscaler 800 V ramps 2027+ with Navitas content; GM >40% + opex discipline; litigation dismissals; Powerchip transition smooth; grid/SST SiC wins convert.

Risks: Content goes to Infineon/ST/TI/Renesas; burn stays high → more ATM; injunction/damages; yield/cost/tariff hit; China EV/industrial soft.

Bull: WBG secular; GaN IC + HV SiC; cash funds the wait; AI power density forces architecture change; stock corrected from $34; CEO executes commercial scale founders couldn't.

Bear: ~70–85x sales for ~$50M run-rate that just shrunk; better-capitalized competition; NVIDIA is a logo not a PO; dilution repeated; legal overhang; foundry transition; abandoning the only volume market they proved (chargers) before proving the next.

Valuation: ~$3.13B / ~$37M TTM ≈ ~85x sales; EV/sales ~70x. Livy Research (Aug 2025) short thesis flagged tariff, dilution, multi-vendor NVIDIA risk — parts already played out; stock nonetheless re-rated on AI hope into 2026.

Bottom line for a holder

You own a speculative WBG call option with real engineering, a fortified cash balance, and a narrative premium that assumes Navitas becomes a meaningful AI/grid power vendor by 2027–28. Near-term fundamentals (sub-$15M quarters, ongoing losses, legal noise, founder exit) do not support a quality-compounder framing. Position size should assume high path-dependency and further volatility (52-week already $7–$34). If holding, demand evidence in reported high-power revenue mix and YoY growth, not more reference-design slides. If adding, prefer weakness after missed conversion of AI samples to production — not FOMO into the logo.

Watch quarterly: high-power/AI+grid mix (company target: AI infrastructure >1/3 of sales by YE2026); Q3'26 vs $13.5M ±0.5M; book-to-bill language; non-GAAP GM ≥39–40%; cash burn; ATM usage despite $557M cash; Powerchip 650 V GaN timing; Wolfspeed/Renesas litigation; named production ramps vs samples; insider Form 4s; China %; restructuring costs.

Verified: SPAC/GeneSiC, FY25/Q2'26 figures, ATM, cash, founder exits, lawsuits. Company claims: design wins, NVIDIA collab, 2027 ramps, AI mix targets. Speculative: bull/bear, long-run share of 800 V sockets.
